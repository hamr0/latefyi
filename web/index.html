Up to 90 days ahead. ISO date or named month (5 May 2026).

What we don't do

No accounts. No analytics. No ads. No third parties.
Your address is kept only while a train is being tracked,
so we can reply. When the trip ends — arrival, STOP, or
cancellation — your address AND the trip record are deleted.
Operator metrics are aggregate only.

Open source. Auditable end-to-end.
